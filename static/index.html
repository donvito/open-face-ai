Face analysis

Detection (YuNet), 5 keypoints, a 478-point 3D face mesh with head pose and expressions (MediaPipe), age group and gender (FairFace), and a 512-d AuraFace embedding for every face in the photo.

Liveness (anti-spoofing)

Silent-Face MiniFASNet (Apache 2.0, two-model ensemble) scores each face as live or presentation attack (printed photo, screen replay). Faces that fail would be skipped for recognition in a production flow. Trained on camera captures, so treat scores on web photos as indicative.

Album clustering

No labels needed: every face across the sample album is embedded, then grouped by similarity. This is how photo apps build "People" albums.

Video privacy

Faces are detected in every frame (YuNet), linked into tracks, recognised per track (AuraFace, top-3 vote), then blurred or mosaicked. Blur everyone, only selected people, or everyone except selected people.

Person tracking

YOLOX finds bodies and YuNet finds faces in each frame. People are matched by face (AuraFace), then by body appearance (colour signature learned from face-confirmed frames) or track continuity when the face is not visible.
